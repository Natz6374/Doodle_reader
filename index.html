Backend

Checking…

Flask serves this page. It finds each character as a connected stroke (merging i-dots and similar), groups characters into words and lines by their gaps, and reads each with a CNN trained on EMNIST handwriting. Inside a word, context settles 0/O, 1/I, 5/S, 8/B, 2/Z and 6/G, and short letters become lowercase.

Works best with printed characters, not joined-up cursive. Every tile shows what the network saw; hover it for the runner-up guesses.
